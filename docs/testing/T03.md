### 1. Contract exercised from a consumer

- base64url round trip, and rejection of non-canonical input (padding, `+/`, whitespace, non-zero trailing bits, bad length, non-ASCII)
- `isFrame` on a valid max-bucket frame; rejection of an extra key, `__proto__`, a wrong ct length, seq 0 and other bad seqs
- `buildAad` output and its validation
- `paddingBucket` edges
- the constants against `docs/architecture.md` (96 KiB, idle 9/10 min, 2 h cap, 15 min TTL)
